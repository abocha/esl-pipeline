### 📚 Growth Timeline & Explored Topics

**Lesson 1 (Baseline & Foundations)**

- **Topics Explored:** Personal introductions, professions, hobbies, travel experiences, ordering at a cafe. Practice text revolved around a "Spy" theme (Bob the spy, secret agencies, gadgets, weapons, martini vs. soda).
- **Grammar Mastered/Reviewed:**
  - **The Core Function of Articles:** Using articles to identify nouns in a sentence.
  - **A/An (Indefinite):** Used for singular, countable nouns. The concept of "attaching a label" to something being introduced for the first time.
  - **The (Definite):** Used when context is shared, when referencing a previously labeled item, or for globally unique things (the sun, the sky, the floor, the door).
  - **Zero Article:** Used for plural nouns (general), uncountable nouns (water, sand, money, time, information), and meals (for dinner, for breakfast).
  - **Countable vs. Uncountable:** Discussed tricky words (money and time are uncountable; weapons, beans, and people are countable; coffee can be both depending on context).
  - **Determiners/Quantifiers:** Understanding that words like _every, each, some, any, another, my, this, that, one_ act as determiners and replace articles (e.g., _every night_ NOT _the every night_).
