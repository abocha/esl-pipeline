## 4. 🛠️ Language Toolkit: Useful Language
### A. 👀 Notice the Grammar (The "Forbidden Mix")
*Tolik, this is the most important part of the homework! Keep these two columns separate.*

:::column-list
    :::column
    **🟢 RULE 1: WANT (+ to)**
    *We use "to" connecting two verbs here.*
    
    *   ✅ He **wants to** go.
    *   ✅ She **wants to** sleep.
    *   ✅ Barsik **wants to** eat.
    *   ❌ *He wants go.* (Missing 'to')
    :::
    :::column
    **🔴 RULE 2: CAN (NO to)**
    *Never use "to" after Can/Can't.*
    
    *   ✅ He **can** go.
    *   ✅ She **can** sleep.
    *   ✅ Barsik **can** jump.
    *   ❌ *He can to go.* (The Forbidden Mix!)
    *   ❌ *She cans go.* (No 's' on Can)
    :::
:::

### B. 🗣️ Useful Chunks & Vocabulary
| Word/Chunk | Meaning | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Independent** 🦁 | Strong, doing things alone (not needing help). | "Cats are usually very **independent**." |
| **Loyal** 🤝 | Always staying with you; a good friend. | "Dogs are very **loyal** to their family." |
| **On a leash** 🐕‍🦺 | The rope used to walk a dog. | "Zaika cannot run away because she is **on a leash**." |
| **Tricks** 🎪 | Special actions a dog learns (sit, jump, paw). | "She has a big brain, so she can do many **tricks**." |
| **Run away** 🏃💨 | To escape; to leave home quickly. | "The dog **wants to run away**." |

## 6. ✅ Your Turn: Complete the Mission!
* **Task:** Tell me about **Barsik** (your cat) or the **Neighbor's Corgi**.
* **Instructions:** Record a short audio (30-60 seconds) or write 5-6 sentences.
* **You must use:**
    1. One sentence with **Can** (e.g., *He can...*)
    2. One sentence with **Cannot** (e.g., *He cannot...*)
    3. One sentence with **Wants to** (e.g., *He wants to...*)
    4. One sentence with **Present Simple** describing a routine (e.g., *He wakes up...*)

**Example (About a lazy cat):**
*"My cat is lazy. He **wants to** sleep all day. He **can** run fast, but he **doesn't want to** run. He **eats** fish every morning."*

## 7. 🧠 Why This Mission Helps You
You are very logical, but English has two different "logic systems" for these verbs. **System A (Want)** uses "to". **System B (Can)** does not use "to". This homework forces your brain to separate these systems so you don't mix them when speaking to colleagues.
