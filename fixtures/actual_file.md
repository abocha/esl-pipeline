## 4. 🛠️ Language Toolkit: Useful Language
### A. 👀 Notice the Grammar: The "Snapshot" Logic

:::column-list
:::column
**1. The Snapshot (Continuous)**
*Logic: Pausing time at a specific moment.*
`Target_Time = 5:00 PM`
`Status = In_Progress`

*   **Past:** "At 5 PM, I **was working**." (I started before 5, finished after 5).
*   **Future:** "At 10 AM, I **will be working**."
*   *Analogy:* Looking at a photograph. The action is frozen in the middle.
:::
:::column
**2. The Interruption (Simple)**
*Logic: An event that interrupts the process.*
`Event.Trigger()`

*   **Structure:** `While [Long Action], [Short Action].`
*   "While I **was waiting**, my phone **rang**."
*   "The screen **froze** while I **was typing**."
*   *Analogy:* A notification popping up on your screen.
:::
:::

### B. 🗣️ Useful Chunks & Vocabulary
*(Retained concepts and fixes from your recent lesson)*

| Term | Meaning & Context |
| :--- | :--- |
| **To freeze / Froze** | 🧊 When a screen/app stops moving. *Ex: "My laptop froze."* (Not "It iced"). |
| **To respond** | 🗣️ To answer (people) or to react (software). *Ex: "The app is not responding."* |
| **To behave** | ⚙️ How a system acts (good or bad). *Ex: "The script is behaving strangely."* |
| **Meanwhile** | 🕰️ At the same time (in a different place/thread). |
| **Reply vs. Replica** | 🚫 *Replica* = A copy. ✅ *Reply* = An answer in a dialogue. |

## 5. ✍️ Practice & Pronunciation
### A. 🔩 Controlled Practice
*(Fill in the gaps. Decide if the action is a **Background Process** (Continuous) or a **Single Event** (Simple).)*

1.  Yesterday at 6 PM, I **___________** (play) a video game.
2.  While I **___________** (code), the electricity **___________** (go) out.
3.  Don't call me at 9 AM tomorrow. I **___________** (sleep).
4.  **[Conditional]** If the server **___________** (crash) again, I will be very angry. *(Hint: No 'will' in If-part!)*
5.  My computer is **___________** (behave) weirdly today.
6.  **[Vocab]** I clicked the button, but the program is not **___________** (respond/reply).
7.  **[Review]** I have **a** new laptop. It works **___________** (good/well).

### B. 🤔 Comprehension Check
1.  What was Alex doing when the screen froze?
2.  What happened while he was waiting?
3.  What will Alex be doing tomorrow at 10:00 AM?

### C. 🎙️ Pronunciation Micro-Focus (≈1 min)
:::callout 🗣️
**Target:** The "NG" Sound (/ŋ/) vs. "N" (/n/).

**The Logic:**
*   **/n/ (Nikolai):** Tongue touches the **front** teeth.
*   **/ŋ/ (King):** Tongue touches the **back** of the mouth (soft palate).

**Action:** Shadow these lines. Feel the difference between the front and back of your mouth.

1.  **Ran** (Front) ... **Rang** (Back).
2.  **Win** (Front) ... **Wing** (Back).
3.  "I was **thinking** about **working** this mor**ning**." (Focus on the *back* position).

**Deliverable:** Send a voice note of sentence #3.
:::

## 6. ✅ Your Turn: Complete the Mission!
* **Task:** Describe your timeline.
    1.  **The Past:** Pick a specific time yesterday (e.g., 8 PM). What **were you doing**?
    2.  **The Interruption:** Did anything happen while you were doing that? (e.g., "My friend called," "I got hungry").
    3.  **The Future:** Pick a specific time tomorrow (e.g., 11 AM). What **will you be doing**?

* **Example:** *"Yesterday at 8 PM, I **was watching** Arcane. While I **was watching**, my mom **called** me. Tomorrow at 11 AM, I **will be sitting** in a lecture."*

* **Plus Task (Optional):** Record this story. Be careful not to say "will" in any *If* clauses if you add one!

## 7. 🧠 Why This Mission Helps You
You often need to describe **processes** (debugging, testing, running code). Using the Continuous tense (`be` + `-ing`) allows you to describe the *state* of the system at a specific timestamp, rather than just stating a fact. It adds precision to your technical storytelling.
