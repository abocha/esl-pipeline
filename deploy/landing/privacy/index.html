Privacy Policy

Draft dated 20 October 2026 · No effective date has been set.

Draft for review. This document describes the implemented service and proposed data-use commitments. It has not been approved as an effective policy. Operator identity, privacy responsibilities, retention, and applicable legal requirements must be settled before publication for production OAuth.

About this service and contact

ESL Pipeline is an invite-only teaching workspace for tutors. It organizes student records, lesson sources, homework, scheduling, and recorded payments. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@eslpipeline.com. The legal name of the service operator remains to be confirmed.

Information handled by the service

  • Accounts and workspaces: account email, password authentication records, workspace membership and permissions, and session and integration authorization records.
  • Teaching records: student names and contact details supplied by tutors, learning preferences, tutor notes, lesson history, recordings, transcripts and speaker labels, learner profiles and learning observations, homework drafts, generated materials, and published handouts and audio.
  • Scheduling and billing records: lesson times and status, student associations, rates, credits, and payment amounts, currencies, dates and notes entered by tutors. Recording a payment in ESL Pipeline does not itself process a bank or card transaction.
  • Technical records: requests, errors, job and integration status, usage and operational logs. Cookies support sessions and authorization; browser storage holds preferences and workflow recovery information, including Calendar setup drafts. The reviewed app and landing do not include advertising or analytics trackers.

Google Calendar access, use, storage, and sharing

Connecting Google Calendar is separate from signing in to ESL Pipeline. A workspace owner or administrator authorizes a Google account, then selects an existing writable lesson calendar. Google supplies the account identifier and verified email to identify that connection. ESL Pipeline lists writable subscribed calendars, including their names and time zones, so you can choose one.

The requested permissions are openid, email, calendar.calendarlist.readonly, and calendar.events (the Calendar scopes use the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/ prefix). Google's event permission covers events across your calendars. ESL Pipeline's integration operates on the selected calendar; use a dedicated lesson calendar without unrelated personal events.

The integration reads event identifiers, titles, times, time zones, recurrence and occurrence information, status, event links, attendee emails and responses, and private association metadata. Synchronization can read events on the selected calendar even when they have not been associated with a student. Associated events become lesson scheduling records. ESL Pipeline creates lesson events and recurring series when requested; timing and recurrence changes are managed in Google Calendar and synchronized back to ESL Pipeline.

When you choose to send an invitation, ESL Pipeline sends the event and the selected student's email to Google. Google distributes invitations and updates according to Calendar behavior. Event information may be visible to invitees and people who can access that calendar. Check recipients and calendar sharing before sending.

ESL Pipeline stores Google account and calendar identifiers, the connected email, granted scopes, encrypted access and refresh credentials, synchronization cursors, event and lesson associations, scheduling fields, and operation records, which can include event observations and invitation recipients. Refresh credentials allow background synchronization when you are not actively using the app. Workspace permissions control access to related teaching and scheduling information.

Proposed Limited Use commitment: ESL Pipeline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data will be used for the visible scheduling and related lesson-history features. It will not be sold or used for advertising, credit decisions, or training generalized AI models. Transfers and human access will be limited to those permitted by that policy, including providing the authorized features, security needs, and legal obligations. These commitments require operator approval before this draft becomes effective.

Connected providers and published materials

Teaching workflows send relevant content to configured external services. The implementation supports OpenAI for assignment and learner-context generation, ElevenLabs for recording transcription, and Inworld, ElevenLabs, or Google for speech generation. Depending on the task, submitted data can include lesson audio, transcripts, tutor instructions, learner context, and text to be spoken. These providers are distinct from Google Calendar authorization.

Database, file or object storage, hosting, and backup systems hold service data. Which providers, regions, contractual safeguards, and international transfers apply to production still require confirmation; this draft makes no claim about their retention or training policies.

Publishing creates shareable handout and audio URLs. People who receive those URLs may access or forward them. Separately, authorizing a connected assistant allows it to read the supported learner teaching context; that disclosure is controlled through a separate authorization flow. Review materials and permissions before sharing.

Retention, disconnecting, and deletion requests

The service retains teaching records, transcripts, generated materials, scheduling history, and operational records. No uniform retention period or automatic account-erasure process has been established. Archiving a student or disconnecting an integration is not deletion.

Recording audio becomes unavailable through the ordinary playback workflow after source confirmation. Physical cleanup is separately configured and is disabled by default; the configurable recovery period is not a promise that all recordings are erased after a fixed number of days. Transcripts and downstream teaching records have a separate lifecycle. Backups, published copies, and provider-held data may outlast changes in the app.

Disconnect Google in Calendar connection settings to stop ESL Pipeline scheduling and sync. This keeps stored credentials and historical records; it does not revoke the grant at Google or delete Google events. To revoke Google's permission, visit your Google Account connections. Revocation does not erase information already stored in ESL Pipeline.

For access, correction, or deletion requests, email hello@eslpipeline.com with enough information to identify the account or relevant tutor. Do not send passwords or tokens. Identity checks, the applicable rights process, retention exceptions, and backup handling remain to be approved; no deletion deadline is promised by this draft.

Student data and recordings

Tutors supply student information and lesson sources. They need appropriate authority to share that information, record participants, and use connected processing services. The allocation of privacy responsibilities between tutors and the operator, including handling data about minors, remains unresolved. This draft does not assert that student or guardian consent has already been obtained.

Protection and changes

The implementation uses authentication, workspace access checks, and encryption for stored Google credentials. This is not a guarantee that every category of data is encrypted at rest or that the service is risk-free. Production security controls and legal obligations need separate confirmation.

An approved policy will need an effective date and a process for notifying users of material changes. New uses of Google data require updated disclosures and consent where required before that use begins.
