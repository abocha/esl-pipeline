Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

About ESL Pipeline

ESL Pipeline is an independent, unregistered project operated by an individual. It provides a free, invite-only teaching workspace for a small alpha of tutors in Russia and Vietnam. Contact hello@eslpipeline.com for privacy questions or requests.

Information we process and why

We process account and workspace details, authentication records, student names and contact details, tutor notes, learning preferences, lesson recordings and transcripts, learner profiles, homework, generated audio and handouts, and lesson and payment records. We use these to provide the teaching workflow, manage access, and maintain the service. Payment records document tutor-entered transactions; ESL Pipeline does not collect those payments.

Session and authorization cookies, browser preferences and workflow recovery storage, and technical and usage logs support access, recovery and troubleshooting. The app and public site do not include advertising or analytics trackers.

Student information and connected services

Tutors decide what learner information to submit. They are responsible for having authority to share it, record lessons, provide relevant notices, and obtain necessary student or guardian permissions. ESL Pipeline remains responsible for its own processing, access controls and safeguards.

Connected transcription, AI generation and speech services receive relevant recordings, transcripts, tutor instructions, learner context or text to be spoken. Hosting, storage and backup services hold service data. Connected services have their own terms and data controls; ESL Pipeline does not control individual ChatGPT users' training settings.

Published handout and audio links can be accessed and forwarded by people who receive them. OpenAI API receives selected teaching context for AI-assisted learning features. Calendar-derived lesson timing helps select and order that context. Memory rebuilds and profile refreshes can also send the completed lesson start time and internal lesson associations to OpenAI API. Separately authorized ChatGPT connections receive selected learner context, rather than raw Calendar events or Google credentials. Review content and permissions before sharing.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google is separate from signing in to ESL Pipeline. We receive the Google account identifier and verified email, list writable subscribed calendars and their names and time zones, and let a workspace owner or administrator select a lesson calendar.

We request openid, email, calendar.calendarlist.readonly and calendar.events; Calendar scopes use the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/ prefix. Google's event permission covers all your calendars; ESL Pipeline uses the selected calendar. Choose a dedicated lesson calendar without unrelated personal events.

We read event identifiers, titles, times, time zones, recurrence, status, links, attendee emails and responses, and private association metadata. Sync can read unassociated events on that calendar. Associated events become lesson records. We create events and recurring series when requested; changes made in Google Calendar synchronize back. If you choose invitations, Google receives the event and student's email and distributes invitations and updates. Invitees and people with calendar access may see event information.

We store account and calendar identifiers, email, granted scopes, encrypted access and refresh credentials, sync cursors, event associations, scheduling fields and operation records, including event observations and invitation recipients. Refresh credentials support background synchronization. Related records are available through workspace permissions and separately authorized learner-context sharing.

Limited Use: ESL Pipeline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. This includes Google-derived information. We use it for visible scheduling, lesson history and authorized learner-context features, not advertising, sale, credit decisions or training generalized AI models. Transfers and human access are limited to what that policy permits.

Retention and your choices

Production recording-audio cleanup is enabled and removes eligible source audio under the configured lifecycle. Audio becomes unavailable through ordinary playback after source confirmation. Physical deletion timing varies; there is no universal deletion deadline. Transcripts, teaching history and generated materials have separate retention and are not erased by source-audio cleanup. Backups, provider-held data and shared copies may remain separately.

Disconnect Google stops local scheduling and sync but retains stored credentials and history. It does not revoke Google's permission or delete events. Revoke access separately in Google Account connections. Revocation does not erase records already stored in ESL Pipeline. Archiving a student is also not deletion.

For access, correction or deletion requests, email hello@eslpipeline.com with the relevant account or tutor details. Do not send passwords or tokens. Requests are handled individually; no fixed deletion deadline or automatic account-erasure process is promised.

Safeguards and policy changes

We use authentication, workspace access controls and encryption for stored Google credentials. No system is risk-free. Material changes will be described on this page. New uses of Google data require updated disclosures and any required consent before they begin.
