The service

ESL Pipeline is an invite-only alpha teaching workspace. It helps tutors organize student information and lesson sources, prepare AI-assisted learning materials, publish handouts and audio, schedule lessons through Google Calendar, and record teaching credits and payments. Features depend on configuration and connected providers. For questions, contact hello@eslpipeline.com. The legal operator name remains to be confirmed.

AI-assisted output and sharing

Transcription, learner-context generation, homework generation, and speech generation use external services. Their output can be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for a learner. Review transcripts, student context, exercises, answers, and audio before relying on or sharing them. A tutor remains responsible for teaching decisions.

Published handouts and audio use shareable URLs. Review the material before publication and share links only with intended recipients. Forwarded or downloaded copies are outside the app's direct control.

Google Calendar and other integrations

Google Calendar connection authorizes an account for a workspace and a selected writable lesson calendar. Review the permissions and use a dedicated lesson calendar. ESL Pipeline can create events and send invitations when you request them. Manage timing and recurrence in Google Calendar; review synchronization warnings and invitation recipients. Google permissions are broader than ESL Pipeline's selected-calendar use.

Disconnecting stops scheduling and sync but preserves stored records and credentials. You can also revoke permission in Google Account connections. This does not delete events or historical teaching records. Separately authorized assistant integrations can read supported learner context. Third-party services have their own terms and policies.
