The alpha service

ESL Pipeline is an independent, unregistered project operated by an individual. The free, invite-only alpha serves a small group of tutors in Russia and Vietnam. It helps organize learner records, process lesson sources, prepare AI-assisted homework and audio, publish handouts, schedule lessons through Google Calendar, and record credits and payments. Contact hello@eslpipeline.com.

Responsible use

Use only accounts, workspaces and materials you are authorized to access. Keep credentials private. Do not bypass access controls, disrupt the service or submit unlawful material. Tutors are responsible for authority to submit learner data, record participants, provide relevant notices, and obtain necessary student or guardian permissions. ESL Pipeline remains responsible for its own processing and safeguards, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Your materials and generated output

You retain your rights to materials you submit. You permit ESL Pipeline to store and process them, use connected providers, generate learning materials, and publish or share content you request, solely as needed to operate the service. This does not transfer ownership to ESL Pipeline.

AI output may be inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable. Review transcripts, learner context, homework and audio before using or sharing them. Tutors remain responsible for teaching decisions. Shareable handout and audio links can be forwarded or downloaded.

Integrations and availability

Review Google permissions, the selected lesson calendar and invitation recipients. Disconnect stops local sync but retains credentials and history; Google revocation is separate. See the Privacy Policy for details. Connected providers have their own terms and policies.

The alpha can change and may experience errors, interruptions or integration failures. Check schedules and generated materials before relying on them. No uptime or uninterrupted-access guarantee is offered. The alpha has no subscription charge or automatic renewal. Payment records do not collect money from students.
