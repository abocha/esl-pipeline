# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ESL instructors, operators, and maintainers use this system while turning lesson recordings,
transcripts, prompts, and drafts into student-ready assignments. They are often checking evidence,
following a workflow, and deciding the next safe action for a student or lesson.

## Product Purpose

ESL Pipeline coordinates an assignment-production workflow from recording intake through transcript,
prompt, draft, publish, and follow-up surfaces. The interface should help operators understand job
state, preserve provenance, recover from blockers, and complete lesson-linked assignment work without
guessing what the backend or pipeline is doing.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, teacherly.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, playful edtech cartoons, dark developer-tool aesthetics, and dense
enterprise admin panels. The product should not feel decorative, gamified, or like a log viewer with
buttons attached.

## Design Principles

- Make pipeline state legible before asking for action.
- Keep operator choices narrow, explicit, and reversible where possible.
- Preserve student and lesson context close to the work being done.
- Use quiet confidence: informative hierarchy, restrained emphasis, and clear feedback.
- Prefer practical teaching-workflow language over platform jargon.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA for product surfaces. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus states, readable
contrast, reduced-motion alternatives, and layouts that remain usable for instructors scanning dense
lesson or student context under time pressure.
