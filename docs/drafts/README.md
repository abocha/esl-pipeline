# Drafts

## Meta

- Audience: maintainers and agents with an explicitly scoped exploratory draft.
- Read Time: 1 minute.
- Authority Level: non-authoritative workspace.
- Last Verified Against: 2026-08-12 repository lifecycle.
- Owner: draft author until the draft is resolved.
- Review Cadence: at the end of the related exploration.
- Status: `draft`.

## Scope and Lifecycle

`docs/drafts/**` is excluded from normal current-task routing and is disposable by default. A draft
cannot establish current behavior, product direction, procedure, or a durable plan.

When resolving a draft, promote durable conclusions to their real owner, archive it only when its
historical rationale is useful, and otherwise delete it. Do not leave an unresolved draft as a
substitute for a plan: use [`docs/plans/README.md`](../plans/README.md) for durable active planning.

The pre-overhaul draft corpus was resolved as historical rather than current material and moved to
[`docs/archive/drafts/`](../archive/drafts/README.md). New drafts belong here only while they remain
explicitly active and scoped.
