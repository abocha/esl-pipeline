## **3. 🛠️ Grammar Profile**

### **✅ Mastered / Solid**

- **Adverbs of Frequency:** Placement (`I usually go...`) is generally correct.
- **Basic Sentence Structure:** SVO order is stable.
- **Verb Patterns:** Distinction between `want to` and `can` (no `to`) is understood.
- **Pronouns:** `Everyone/Everybody` treated as singular (self-corrects this now).

### **⚠️ In Progress / Needs Spiral Review**

- **Past Simple Irregular Verbs:** Knows the theory, but recall is slow (`go` -> `went`, `see` -> `saw`). Needs drill.
- **Articles:** Concept of "Specific vs. General" is understood, but she occasionally misses articles with "uncountable" concepts in Russian logic (e.g., _a_ wild rat, _the_ neighborhood).
- **Speech Verbs:** Theoretically understands `Say` vs `Tell`, but struggles to choose correctly in spontaneous speech.
- **Noun + Noun:** Recently introduced (e.g., _Carrot cake_ vs _Cake of carrots_). Still internalizing.

### **🚨 Critical Error Zones (Fossilized)**

- **3rd Person `-s`:** Still drops this in free speech ("My husband _wake_ up"). **Requires constant gentle correction.**
- **Indirect Questions:** Tends to use Russian word order for complex questions (e.g., _"Where do they parents live?"_ instead of _"Where do her parents live?"_ or _"I don't know where they live"_).
- **Reflexive Verbs:** "I decide _myself_" (interference from Russian _решаю сама_).

## **4. 🗣️ Pronunciation & Phonology Report**

- **The "Silent E" Rule:** Major recent focus. She tends to pronounce `-ed` or ending vowels as separate syllables.
  - _Error:_ `Tired` pronounced /ta-i-red/ (3 syllables).
  - _Target:_ /taɪrd/ (1 syllable).
- **Vowel Discrimination (/æ/ vs /e/):**
  - _Error:_ Confusing `Rat` (her pet) with `Red` (color).
  - _Target:_ "Smiling A" for Rat/Cat/Bad vs "Relaxed E" for Red/Bed.
- **Consonants:**
  - **V vs W:** Confusing `Invite` (/w/) vs `Winter` (/v/).
  - **K vs C:** Pronouncing `Coat` as `Count` and `Key` as `Kay`.
