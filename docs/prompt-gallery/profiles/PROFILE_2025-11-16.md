## **3. 🛠️ Grammar Profile**

### **✅ Mastered / Solid**
*   **Adverbs of Frequency:** Placement (`I usually go...`) is generally correct.
*   **Basic Sentence Structure:** SVO order is stable.
*   **Verb Patterns:** Distinction between `want to` and `can` (no `to`) is understood.
*   **Pronouns:** `Everyone/Everybody` treated as singular (self-corrects this now).

### **⚠️ In Progress / Needs Spiral Review**
*   **Past Simple Irregular Verbs:** Knows the theory, but recall is slow (`go` -> `went`, `see` -> `saw`). Needs drill.
*   **Articles:** Concept of "Specific vs. General" is understood, but she occasionally misses articles with "uncountable" concepts in Russian logic (e.g., *a* wild rat, *the* neighborhood).
*   **Speech Verbs:** Theoretically understands `Say` vs `Tell`, but struggles to choose correctly in spontaneous speech.
*   **Noun + Noun:** Recently introduced (e.g., *Carrot cake* vs *Cake of carrots*). Still internalizing.

### **🚨 Critical Error Zones (Fossilized)**
*   **3rd Person `-s`:** Still drops this in free speech ("My husband *wake* up"). **Requires constant gentle correction.**
*   **Indirect Questions:** Tends to use Russian word order for complex questions (e.g., *"Where do they parents live?"* instead of *"Where do her parents live?"* or *"I don't know where they live"*).
*   **Reflexive Verbs:** "I decide *myself*" (interference from Russian *решаю сама*).

## **4. 🗣️ Pronunciation & Phonology Report**
*   **The "Silent E" Rule:** Major recent focus. She tends to pronounce `-ed` or ending vowels as separate syllables.
    *   *Error:* `Tired` pronounced /ta-i-red/ (3 syllables).
    *   *Target:* /taɪrd/ (1 syllable).
*   **Vowel Discrimination (/æ/ vs /e/):**
    *   *Error:* Confusing `Rat` (her pet) with `Red` (color).
    *   *Target:* "Smiling A" for Rat/Cat/Bad vs "Relaxed E" for Red/Bed.
*   **Consonants:**
    *   **V vs W:** Confusing `Invite` (/w/) vs `Winter` (/v/).
    *   **K vs C:** Pronouncing `Coat` as `Count` and `Key` as `Kay`.
