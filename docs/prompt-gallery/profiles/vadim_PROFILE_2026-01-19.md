## 4. 📊 Proficiency Map

### ✅ Strengths (Leverage These)
*   **Complex Vocabulary:** Uses terms like *distributed systems, optimization, native, built-in, cliffhanger, ecosystem* correctly.
*   **Conceptual Grammar:** Grasps logic-based grammar quickly (Conditionals, Emphatic 'Do', Passive Voice).
*   **Fluency in Tech Contexts:** Can describe how a Python scraper works or why banking servers need redundancy without hesitation.

### ⚠️ Fossilized Errors (The "Bug List")
*   **Articles (Critical):** Consistently omits `a/an` before singular countable nouns (*"It is theoretical subject," "It is free game"*). **Action:** Needs constant "Stop & Correct."
*   **Plurals:** Often uses singular nouns after numbers or quantifiers (*"Two exam," "A lot of child"*).
*   **Comparatives:** Frequent slips into **"More better"** or "More fast."
*   **Pronunciation of "-ed":** Tendency to pronounce the `e` in words like *tired*, *watched*, *passed*.

### 🔄 In Progress (Current Targets)
*   **Narrative Tenses:** Moving from "Then I go and I watch" to "Then I **went** and I **watched**." He is improving but slips back to Present Simple when telling stories.
*   **Preposition Stranding:** Learning to say "The tool I rely **on**" instead of "The tool on which I rely."
*   **Emphatic Structures:** Just learned `I do use` and `Indeed`. Needs practice to make it natural.
