# 🧠 Foundational Student Profile: Veronika
**Version:** 5.0
**Date:** January 13, 2026

## A. General Snapshot
*   **Student:** Veronika (14 years old, Russian-speaking background).
*   **Current Level:** A2+ (Strong Pre-Intermediate).
*   **Personality:** Methodical, diligent, and introverted. She is reserved when discussing her personal life (often gives short, functional answers) but opens up when discussing **art** (she draws/mixes styles), **video games** (Animal Crossing), or **third-person narratives**.
*   **Learning Mode:** She responds best to **structure** and **analytical explanations** (e.g., understanding the "vibe" of a tense rather than just memorizing rules). She struggles with spontaneous open-ended questions ("Why do you like this?") but excels at descriptive tasks ("Describe this picture").

## B. Critical Pedagogical Focus (The "Big 3")
These are the active friction points to address in every assignment:

1.  **🚨 Pronunciation of `-ed` Endings:** This is a persistent, fossilized error. She often adds an extra syllable (`play-id` instead of `playd`) or confuses /t/ and /d/. **Action:** Every homework must have a micro-drill on this.
2.  **Verb Patterns (The "Two Verbs" Rule):** Recently introduced (Jan 6). She is learning to distinguish between **Team TO** (`want to go`) and **Team ING** (`enjoy going`). She needs repetition to automate this without pausing.
3.  **Prepositions of Technology:** She has a habit of saying "in the Internet" or "in the phone" (Russian interference). **Action:** Drill **ON** for screens/platforms.

*   **Outcome:** She grasped the theoretical difference well but needs practice to apply it automatically.

## F. Recommendations for the Assistant

1.  **Avoid Personal "Why" Questions:** She dislikes explaining her feelings deepy (e.g., "Why do you like art?"). **Strategy:** Ask "How" or "What" questions, or ask her to describe a third-party character (e.g., "Why does *Alex* like art?").
2.  **Gamify the "Drills":** Use the context of "Animal Crossing" or "The Mystery Game" (Alias) to practice grammar. She prefers this to dry exercises.
3.  **Strict Pronunciation Formatting:** In the "Pronunciation" section of homework, always categorize words by sound (/t/, /d/, /ɪd/) to help her visualize the `-ed` rule.
4.  **Next Logical Step:**
    *   **Writing Task:** A short bio of a character using *Present Continuous* for temporary states ("He is living in a tent") and *Verb Patterns* for likes/dislikes ("He enjoys fishing").
