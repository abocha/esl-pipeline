## Source-review limits and corrected audit inference

The initial investigation overstated the voice-alignment finding. The readiness response **does**
expose `validationFailures`, `strictMismatches`, provider-error and nullable selection fields under
`voiceAlignment`. Backend job/error classifications and the readiness source map own those values.
The removed validation/shadow metric-stream names are a separate concern: current orchestrator
telemetry emits selection/provider-error streams through an injected adapter, and the backend's
default metrics facade is a no-op. The revised runbook distinguishes the two surfaces.

Product intent remains binding where recorded; source presence establishes implementation rather
than activation, cohort readiness or completion of product gates. CLI and migration presence do
not establish residual learner-data eligibility or retirement. Current product/cohort decisions
belong in Linear; a future authorized operation must obtain its own deployment and data evidence.
This revision performs no provider, production or learner-data probes and claims no deployment.
